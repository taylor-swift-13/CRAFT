\section{Conclusion and Limitations}\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ============================================================

LoopGym makes filtered clause composition the inference target: RCF combines
rollouts, SFT distills their union, and target-independent RL rewards useful
clauses and complementary coverage.

Evidence is limited to scalar-integer, single-loop C programs, Frama-C/WP and
ACSL, and vLLM-served local checkpoints.  Credit also filters rollouts
separately whereas deployment filters their union.  Appendices~\ref{app:limitations}
and~\ref{app:houdini-order} detail these scope and reward approximations.
