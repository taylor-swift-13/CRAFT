\section{Conclusion and Limitations}\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ============================================================

Useful invariant clauses need not occur in one successful response.
Cross-response filtering substantially improves bare-model verification,
while SFT and RL make these gains available at smaller sampling budgets.
Reward ablations show that whole-response credit can improve pass while
reducing compositional verification.  Projected pass estimates and prefix composition
results use different aggregation at the same nominal budget, so sampling
variance can affect measured gaps and saturation trends.  We leave extending
CRAFT beyond loop verification—to testcase generation and function contract
generation—for future study (Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}).
