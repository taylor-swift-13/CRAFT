\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
%% ============================================================

\tightparagraph{Invariant generation and verified filtering.}
Classical invariant synthesis uses abstract interpretation, algebraic relations,
constraint solving, interpolation, recurrences, syntax-guided search, or
counterexamples~\citep{cousot1977abstract,cousot1978automatic,karr1976affine,
colon2003linear,sankaranarayanan2004non,gulwani2008program,gupta2009invgen,
mcmillan2003interpolation,kincaid2017compositional,alur2013sygus,garg2014ice};
dynamic approaches mine likely invariants from executions~\citep{daikon}.
Verified filtering retains an inductive subset of proposed
clauses~\citep{flanagan2001houdini}.  Neural and LLM systems learn construction
policies or combine generation with solver feedback, static analysis,
filtering, and repair
~\citep{si2018learning,si2020code2inv,ryan2020cln2inv,yao2020gcln,
yu2023lipus,kamath2023finding,chakraborty2023ranking,
liu2024acinv,
wu2024lemur}. Baldur instead generates and repairs whole proofs
~\citep{first2023baldur}. LaM4Inv conjoins BMC-filtered predicates
from candidate invariants~\citep{wu2024lam4inv}; Clause2Inv searches
conjunctions and disjunctions of clauses~\citep{cao2025clause2inv}.
Both iteratively refine candidates using verification feedback; LORIS repairs
local reasoning errors~\citep{li2026loris}. Quokka uses UAutomizer
to evaluate invariant correctness and verification speedups
~\citep{wei2025invbench}.  These workflows rely on visible targets or
target-derived feedback unavailable under target hiding.
CRAFT jointly filters clauses from independent responses without verifier
feedback to the generator, and aligns SFT and RL with this composition.

Broader specification-generation frameworks also synthesize loop invariants
by combining LLM generation with
program analysis or symbolic verification
~\citep{wen2024enchanting,yang2026sespec,ma2025specgen}.

\tightparagraph{Training from formal and behavioral feedback.}
Verified data and execution or prover feedback have been used to train
invariant, code, and proof models
~\citep{wei2025invbench,pinto2026notall,le2022coderl,yan2025reform}.
Wonda curates verifier-generated invariants through AST normalization,
LLM-driven semantic rewriting, and augmentation to train small language
models~\citep{pinto2026notall}.
COINS and SpecRL further use positive and negative behaviors to distinguish
weak valid specifications from stronger ones
~\citep{yang2026coins,huang2026specrl}; SpecRL rewards verified candidates
according to the fraction of negative tests their specifications reject.
CRAFT instead uses target-hidden behavioral evidence to train on verified
multi-response compositions and reward retained clauses from failed responses.

\tightparagraph{Credit assignment in RLVR.}
Related RLVR and RLHF methods study group-relative advantages and
fine-grained credit~\citep{shao2024deepseekmath,li2026ssvpo,cao2025scar}.
Recent methods optimize pass@\(k\) or up-weight rare correct responses
~\citep{walder2025pkpo,chen2025passktraining,he2025unlikeliness}, and
per-response RL can improve pass@1 without expanding large-\(k\) coverage
~\citep{yue2025rlcapacity,he2025unlikeliness}. CRAFT can combine
clauses from multiple failed responses into a proof. Its clause-based coverage
and Shapley credit produce response-level advantages for policy updates.
