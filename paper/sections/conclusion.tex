\section{Conclusion and Limitations}\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ============================================================

CRAFT aligns training with compositional inference: its pipeline composes
rollouts, SFT distills their union, and target-independent RL rewards useful
responses through their pooled survivors while discounting repeated coverage.  On
Qwen3-8B, the pipeline alone raises one-response verification from 6.43\% to
37.86\% (+31.43 points; +488.8\%).  Rejection-filtered SFT raises compose@1
to 65.50\%, and the subsequent compositional RL stage reaches 69.23\%.
Together they deliver a 62.80-point (976.7\%) end-to-end gain, reduce the SFT
checkpoint's verification error by 10.8\%, and exceed Claude Sonnet-4.6 at
compose@1 by 12.26 points.
Whole-response RL sharpens pass@1 while collapsing compose@32; decomposed
credit preserves partial candidates, and group credit rewards complementary
coverage, mitigating the corresponding exploration collapse in GRPO.

The interface extends to reusable atoms that can be pooled and soundly
filtered.  Specification and test-case generation are potential instances,
with task-specific operators, verifiers, and signals; Appendix~\ref{app:formal-method}
states the formal conditions.

Evidence is limited to scalar-integer single-loop C programs, Frama-C/WP,
ACSL, and the evaluated checkpoints.  Provenance credit is conditioned on the
sampled group rather than exact coalition Shapley values;
Appendices~\ref{app:limitations} and~\ref{app:houdini-order} give the
evaluation scope and pooled-credit measurements.
