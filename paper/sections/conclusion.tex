\section{Conclusion and Limitations}\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ============================================================

CRAFT makes filtered clause composition the inference target: its pipeline
composes
rollouts, SFT distills their union, and target-independent RL rewards useful
clauses and complementary coverage.

The recipe transfers beyond loop invariants.  It applies to problems whose
answers (i) decompose into independently checkable units composed by
conjunction, (ii) admit a cheap mechanical filter for unit validity,
(iii) include valid-but-weak degenerate solutions that pass/fail cannot
grade, and (iv) allow behavioral negatives, on which weak and strong
solutions disagree, to be derived from executions or simulations.  Test
generation judged by mutant killing, auxiliary-lemma synthesis for proof
assistants, and data-constraint discovery all satisfy these conditions.

Evidence is limited to scalar-integer, single-loop C programs, Frama-C/WP and
ACSL, and vLLM-served local checkpoints.  Credit also filters rollouts
separately whereas inference filters their union.  Appendices~\ref{app:limitations}
and~\ref{app:houdini-order} detail these scope and reward approximations.
