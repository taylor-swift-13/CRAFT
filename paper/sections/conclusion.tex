\section{Conclusion and Limitations}\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ============================================================

Our results show that useful invariant clauses need not occur in a single
successful response.  Filtering and combining clauses across responses
substantially improves bare-model verification, while SFT and RL make these
gains available at smaller sampling budgets.  RL continues to improve
compose@1 after SFT, despite little change at larger \(k\).  The reward
ablations further show that higher pass does not necessarily imply better
composition: clause and group credit retain compositional gains that are lost
under whole-response credit.  Future work should test whether the same training
design transfers to other tasks with independently verifiable components
(Appendix~\ref{app:limitations}).
