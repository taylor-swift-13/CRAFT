\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
%% ============================================================

\tightparagraph{Invariant generation and verified filtering.}
Classical invariant synthesis uses abstract interpretation, affine relations,
templates, interpolation, syntax-guided search, or counterexamples
~\citep{cousot1977abstract,karr1976affine,mcmillan2003interpolation,
alur2013sygus,garg2014ice}; Houdini retains an inductive subset of proposed
clauses~\citep{flanagan2001houdini}.  Neural and LLM systems learn construction
policies or combine generation with solver feedback, static analysis,
filtering, and repair
~\citep{si2018learning,si2020code2inv,ryan2020cln2inv,yao2020gcln,
yu2023lipus,kamath2023finding,chakraborty2023ranking,cao2025clause2inv,
wu2024lam4inv,liu2024acinv,wen2024enchanting,ma2024specgen,yang2026sespec,
wu2024lemur,first2023baldur}.  Clause2Inv reuses clauses from previous guesses
and adaptively combines them using SMT counterexamples, including
postcondition-derived feedback~\citep{cao2025clause2inv}.  CRAFT pools clauses
across independent responses before target-independent joint Houdini
filtering.  It couples this composition with training: accepted compositions
become SFT targets, and surviving clauses determine credit for their
proposing responses.

\tightparagraph{Training from formal and behavioral feedback.}
Verified data and execution or prover feedback have been used to train
invariant, code, and proof models
~\citep{wei2025invbench,pinto2026notall,le2022coderl,yan2025reform}.
Wonda curates invariant supervision from verifier output through
normalization and semantic rewriting~\citep{pinto2026notall}.
COINS and SpecRL further use positive and negative behaviors to distinguish
weak valid specifications from stronger ones
~\citep{yang2026coins,huang2026specrl}; SpecRL rewards verified candidates
according to the fraction of negative tests their specifications reject.
CRAFT distills accepted multi-response compositions into single-response
supervision and uses decompositional clause-level credit to reward useful
surviving parts even when the complete response fails.  Its behavioral
evidence comes from target-hidden executions and is applied only after
clauses survive pooled verification.

\tightparagraph{Set-level credit in verifiable-reward RL.}
Per-response RL can improve pass@1 without expanding large-\(k\) coverage
~\citep{yue2025rlcapacity,he2025unlikeliness}.  Recent objectives directly
optimize pass@\(k\) or up-weight rare correct responses
~\citep{walder2025pkpo,chen2025passktraining,he2025unlikeliness}.
Pass@\(k\) objectives seek at least one successful complete response. CRAFT
constructs successful invariants from clauses distributed across responses,
even when no individual response independently proves the target.
It decomposes responses before assigning each response the coverage
of its surviving clauses.
Credit is computed from clauses, while the policy update uses a
response-level advantage.
