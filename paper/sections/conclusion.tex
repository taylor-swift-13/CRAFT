\section{Conclusion and Limitations}\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ============================================================

Useful invariant clauses need not occur in one successful response.
Cross-response filtering substantially improves bare-model verification,
while SFT and RL make these gains available at smaller sampling budgets.
Reward ablations show that whole-response credit can improve pass while
reducing compositional verification. The evaluation focuses on scalar-integer
single-loop programs. Future work will explore extending CRAFT to other tasks
with compositional structure, such as test-case generation and
function-contract generation.
