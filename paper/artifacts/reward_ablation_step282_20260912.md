# 2ep (step282) Reward Ablation Results

Qwen3-8B, GRPO, 2 epochs (~282 steps), data=craft_rl.parquet (9024 programs)

All models use **structured** negative sampler (default).

## Reward variants

| Variant | Formula |
|---------|---------|
| **full** | 1.0×base + 0.3×shapley − 0.02×redundancy − 0.05×overflow |
| **base** | negative coverage only (no shapley/redundancy/overflow) |
| **wc** (whole_coverage) | whole-program coverage score |
| **binary** | 0/1 frama-c verification pass/fail |
