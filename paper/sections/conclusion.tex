\section{Conclusion and Limitations}\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ============================================================

CRAFT recovers proofs from clauses in responses that fail to prove targets
alone. Decomposing, filtering, and composing those clauses raises Base
Qwen3-8B verification from 6.37\% pass@1 to 37.86\% compose@1.
Composed SFT labels broaden coverage, while subsequent RL makes useful
clauses available in fewer samples. Reward ablations show that whole-response
credit can improve standalone pass but reduce verification through
decomposition, filtering, and composition. Our evaluation covers
scalar-integer single-loop programs, conjunctive composition, and Frama-C/WP;
nonlinear relations remain difficult. Future work will extend CRAFT to richer
program states and control flow and test whether inference and training built
around decomposition, filtering, and composition transfer to other
compositional tasks, including specification and test-case generation.
