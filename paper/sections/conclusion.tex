\section{Conclusion and Limitations}\label{sec:conclusion}
%% ============================================================
\enlargethispage{2\baselineskip}

CRAFT recovers proofs from clauses in responses that fail to prove targets
alone. Decomposing, filtering, and composing those clauses raises Base
Qwen3-8B verification from 6.37\% to 37.86\%.
Composed SFT labels broaden coverage, while subsequent RL makes useful
clauses available in fewer samples. Reward ablations show that whole-response
credit can improve standalone pass but reduce compositional verification.
CRAFT currently handles one loop
over scalar integers and combines clauses by conjunction. We evaluate it with
Frama-C/WP; nonlinear relations remain challenging for the evaluated models.
Future work will extend CRAFT to richer program states and control flow
and explore its transfer to specification and test-case generation.
