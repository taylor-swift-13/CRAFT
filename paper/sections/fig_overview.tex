\caption{\textbf{Training and inference execute the same target-hidden
rollout--Houdini core.}
Both form rollout groups, grow a merged pool with target-free feedback, and
retain clauses through the same Frama-C/WP Houdini operator.
Training turns these operations into generation and refinement rewards;
inference instead restores \(Q\) and applies the final proof gate.
The execution sampler supplies training credit but is never called at
inference.}
